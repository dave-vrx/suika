Getting Started

How to Play

One minute to learn, a lifetime to master. Here's everything you need to compete.

01

Aim & drop

Fruits fall from the top. Move with your mouse, arrow keys, or touch to aim, then click or press Space to drop.

02

Match to merge

Two of the same fruit that touch merge into the next size up. Drop matching fruit next to each other to grow big melons.

03

Chain combos

A merge that triggers more merges is a combo — and every link pays bonus points. Plan drops that cause chain reactions.

04

Beat the line

If the stack crosses the red danger line, the round is over. Stack wide, keep the middle clear, and stay under the ceiling.

05

Climb the chain

Cherry → Strawberry → Grape → Lemon → Orange → Apple → Pear → Peach → Pineapple → Melon → Watermelon 🏆. Bigger merges score way more.

06

Leave your name

Hit a top-10 score and the game will ask for your name. Save it, claim your rank, and show up in the hall of melons.
