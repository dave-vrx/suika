Getting Started

How to Play Melons & Chill

New to Suika? Here's everything you need to know before your first drop.

01

Get VRChat & find the world

Download VRChat on PC or Quest, search for “Melons & Chill” in the Worlds menu, and hop in. Grab a comfy seat — the arena is right in front of you.

02

Know the Suika rules

Fruits drop from the top. Drop them so matching fruits touch and they merge into the next size up. Chain small merges into bigger fruits and stack toward the giant watermelon.

03

Don't hit the ceiling

The line at the top is your danger zone. If fruit stacks above it, the round ends. Play tight, stack wide, and keep your board clean.

04

Learn the fruit chain

Cherry → Strawberry → Grape → Dekopon → Orange → Apple → Pear → Peach → Pineapple → Melon → Watermelon. Know your merge tree and plan your drops.

05

Join a SUIKA LEAGUE match

Weekly matches are hosted on the Discord. Show up, get paired, and earn points. Win rounds, gain MMR, and climb the seasonal leaderboard.

06

Practice like a pro

Check the tutorials section for advanced strategy: side-stacking, combo timing, and late-game cleanup to keep your board alive until the big melon.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SUIKA LEAGUE?

SUIKA LEAGUE is a competitive community league for the VRChat Suika game Melons & Chill. We host weekly matches, seasonal leaderboards, and tournaments for melon fans of every skill level.

Do I need to be good at Suika to join?

Nope. The league is split into divisions by skill, and we have plenty of practice lobbies. Join as a rookie and climb as you improve.

Do I need VRChat on PC or is Quest OK?

Both work. Melons & Chill runs on PC and standalone Quest. Desktop mode, VR, and touch controls are all supported.

Is SUIKA LEAGUE free to join?

Yes. The league, tournaments, and all community resources are completely free.

How do I find the world in VRChat?

Open VRChat → Worlds → search “Melons & Chill” → click Enter. You can favorite the world to find it again instantly.

How are league points calculated?

You earn points from match placement and wins. Your best rounds feed into your seasonal MMR, which decides your division and rank.
